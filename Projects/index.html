Battery Safety · General Motors Collaboration

EV Battery Pack Thermal Runaway & Ejecta Mitigation

Developed a full battery-pack CFD model to study high-temperature product-gas and particle transport during multi-cell thermal runaway. The work quantified heat retention, particle deposition, venting, secondary ignition risk, and potential mitigation strategies.
